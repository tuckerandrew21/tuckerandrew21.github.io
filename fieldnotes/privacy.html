Fieldnotes privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-29.

Fieldnotes is used only by its developer, Andrew Tucker, with his own Google account.

Google user data

  • Fieldnotes requests read-only access to Gmail (gmail.readonly) for the developer's own mailbox.
  • It reads only recent messages needed to complete the developer's own website sign-ups, such as one-time verification codes.
  • Message content is used only for that step. It is not stored beyond the step, not shared with or sold to anyone, and not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • The access credential is kept in a private secrets store and can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
